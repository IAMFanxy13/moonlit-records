# Piano sample attribution

The piano audio files in `salamander/` are a compact MP3 subset of **Salamander Grand Piano V3**, recorded by Alexander Holm and distributed under the [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

- Original instrument: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
- Web-optimized files: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander
- Playback library: https://tonejs.github.io/

The samples are used without endorsement by the original author.
